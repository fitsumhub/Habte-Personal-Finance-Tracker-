Security & Privacy

Your Money Data
Belongs to You.

Habte is designed around a privacy-first architecture. Your financial information — every transaction, balance, and account — stays on your device. No server receives it, no account is required to store it, and no third party can access it.

No account required — use the app immediately after install
100% offline — all data stored locally on your device
Biometric lock — fingerprint or face ID on startup
Privacy Mode — prevents screenshots of financial data
Balance masking — hide all amounts with a single tap

Habte reads incoming SMS messages from known bank sender IDs (e.g., "127" for Telebirr, "CBE" for CBE). It uses pattern matching to extract the transaction amount, type (debit/credit), and timestamp — entirely on-device. It does NOT read personal messages.
