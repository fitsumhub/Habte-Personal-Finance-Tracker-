Core Features

Everything You Need.
Nothing You Don't.

Built around how Ethiopian users actually manage money — automatic, private, and comprehensive.

Automatic SMS Tracking

Reads transaction SMS from CBE, Telebirr, BOA, Awash, Dashen, Hibret, and more. Extracts amount, balance, and merchant — no manual entry ever needed.

Budgets & Analytics

Income vs. expenses by week, month, or year. Break spending down by category or by bank. Track net worth across every account in one dashboard.

Private by Design

Your financial data never leaves your device. No cloud account, no login, and no server — stored locally with PIN and biometric lock protecting the app.

New

Achievement Certificates

Celebrate real progress with Weekly, Monthly, and Yearly certificates generated from your actual savings. Downloadable, shareable, with Ethiopian-themed designs.

Payment Reminders

Never miss a bill. Set recurring reminders for rent, subscriptions, and payments with custom lead times and notification settings.

5 Home Screen Widgets

Net Worth, Weekly Spending, Budget Pulse, Quick Tracker, and Multi-Bank balance — beautiful widgets right on your home screen for instant financial awareness.

English + Amharic

Full bilingual interface. Switch between English and Amharic (አማርኛ) at any time, with complete translation of all UI text and categories.

Export & Backup

Export your transaction history as CSV or JSON anytime for external analysis or safekeeping. Restore from backup whenever needed.

Security & Privacy

Your Money Data
Belongs to You.

Habte is designed around a privacy-first architecture. Your financial information — every transaction, balance, and account — stays entirely on your device. No server receives it, no account is required, no third party can access it. SMS parsing happens 100% on-device — nothing is ever uploaded.

No account required — works immediately after install
100% offline — all data stored locally in secure SQLite
Biometric lock — fingerprint or face ID on startup
Privacy Mode — prevents screenshots of financial data
No data-selling, no ads that access your financial data
